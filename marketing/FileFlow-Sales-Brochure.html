01 — Capabilities
Everything teams need

One place for files — fast, friendly, complete

From drag-and-drop uploads of huge files to rich in-browser previews, FileFlow covers the full day-to-day workflow your users expect from a premium cloud tool.

📤

Resumable uploads

Drag-and-drop with chunked, resumable transfers — multi-gigabyte files survive flaky connections.

👁️

Rich previews

Images, video, audio, PDF, Markdown, CSV, Excel, Word & PowerPoint, plus code with syntax highlighting.

🔗

Smart share links

Public links with passwords, expiry dates, and download limits — including one-time links.

👥

Team collaboration

Shared folders and direct user-to-user shares with read or read-write access, all inside the app.

📥

Upload requests

Send a “drop box” link so clients and partners can upload to you — no account required.

🕒

Versions & trash

Automatic file versioning and a 30-day recycle bin mean nothing is ever lost by accident.

🔎

Search & tags

Full-text search, tags, and collections keep large libraries instantly navigable.

🌍

Polished & localized

Light/dark themes, keyboard shortcuts, and English & French out of the box.

02 — Trust
Security by design

Built for data you can’t put in someone else’s cloud

FileFlow ships with the controls security and compliance teams ask for — and the codebase is hardened against the file-sharing attack patterns that matter.

✓

✓
Role-based access controlRoles, groups, granular permissions and per-path restrictions.
✓
Full audit trailEvery login, share and file action logged and exportable to CSV.
✓
Session control & quotasInstant “log out everywhere”, admin revoke, per-user storage limits.
✓
Hardened by defaultStrict CSP, secure cookies, rate-limited logins, path-traversal protection.

Your data stays putSelf-hosted on your infrastructure — no third-party data processors.
